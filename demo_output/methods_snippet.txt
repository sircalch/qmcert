All quantum-chemical calculations were performed using ORCA at the wB97X-D3BJ/def2-TZVP level of theory with D3BJ in CPCM (Water). Calculation quality, SCF convergence, and wavefunction consistency were systematically verified using QMCert v1.0.0 (Monreal-Hernández, 2026). Harmonic vibrational frequencies were calculated to confirm that optimized structures correspond to true local minima on the potential energy surface (0 imaginary frequencies, lowest mode = 45.2 cm$^{-1}$). Thermal free energies were adjusted using Grimme's quasi-RRHO harmonic entropy correction for 2 low-frequency modes below 100 cm$^{-1}$. Overall computational reproducibility was validated with status: PASS.
