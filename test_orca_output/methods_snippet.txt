All quantum-chemical calculations were performed using ORCA at the B3LYP/def2-TZVP level of theory with D3BJ in CPCM(WATER). Calculation quality, SCF convergence, and wavefunction consistency were systematically verified using QMCert v1.0.0 (Monreal-Hernández, 2026). Harmonic vibrational frequencies were calculated to confirm that optimized structures correspond to true local minima on the potential energy surface (0 imaginary frequencies, lowest mode = 0.0 cm$^{-1}$). Overall computational reproducibility was validated with status: PASS.
